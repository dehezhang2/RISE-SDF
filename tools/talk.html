Inverse rendering: from photos to a relightable asset逆渲染：从照片到可重打光的资产

Given multi-view images and camera poses of an object, recover what produced them, so the object can be rendered again under any new light.给定一个物体的多视角图像和相机位姿，恢复生成这些图像的因素，从而在任意新光照下重新渲染它。

Geometry几何

signed distance field \(s(\mathbf x)\); surface \(s=0\), normal \(\mathbf n\)符号距离场 \(s(\mathbf x)\)；表面 \(s=0\)，法线 \(\mathbf n\)

Material材质

albedo \(\mathbf a\), metallic \(m\), roughness \(\rho\) of a simplified Disney BRDF简化 Disney BRDF 的反照率 \(\mathbf a\)、金属度 \(m\)、粗糙度 \(\rho\)

Lighting光照

a distant environment map \(L\)远处的环境贴图 \(L\)

Glossy objects are the hard case: a mirror-like surface shows its surroundings, not itself, so reflections are easily baked into the texture or the shape.高光物体是难点：镜面一样的表面映出的是周围环境而不是自己，反射很容易被错当成纹理或几何。

RISE-SDF at a glanceRISE-SDF 概览

1

Stage 1: geometry first阶段一：先学几何

A neural SDF (NeuS on a progressive hash grid) with a reflection-aware radiance field: diffuse, specular and blend MLPs.神经 SDF（渐进式哈希网格上的 NeuS），配一个感知反射的辐射场：漫反射、高光和混合三个 MLP。

2

Stage 2: information sharing阶段二：信息共享

The same MLP heads also predict albedo and metallic; split-sum PBR with a cube-mipmap environment; trained jointly with the radiance field.同一组 MLP 头同时预测反照率和金属度；用 split-sum PBR 和立方体 mipmap 环境光，与辐射场联合训练。

3

Indirect light间接光

Training: an MLP predicts the indirect specular light. Relighting: a second split-sum traces one bounce.训练时：一个 MLP 预测高光的间接光。重打光时：用第二次 split-sum 追踪一次反弹。

4

A dataset to measure it一个能评测它的数据集

Five glossy scenes with ground-truth albedo, roughness and relighting under nine environment maps.五个高光场景，提供反照率、粗糙度的真值，以及九张环境贴图下的重打光真值。

Geometry: volume rendering a signed distance field几何：对符号距离场做体渲染

NeuS turns the SDF along a camera ray into opacities, so a surface can be learned from images alone. Sharpen the density and the weights collapse onto the first surface.NeuS 把相机光线上的 SDF 转成不透明度，于是只用图像就能学出表面。把密度调尖，权重会集中到第一个表面上。

split-sum
\[\mathbf c\approx\underbrace{\int f_r\,(\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\omega)\,d\boldsymbol\omega}_{\text{BRDF integral: 2D LUT}}\cdot\underbrace{\int L(\boldsymbol\omega)\,p(\boldsymbol\omega;\hat{\mathbf d},\rho)\,d\boldsymbol\omega}_{\text{light integral: mipmap}(\hat{\mathbf d},\rho)}\]

Limitations局限

01

Isotropic BRDF各向同性 BRDF

No transparent or anisotropic materials.处理不了透明或各向异性的材质。

02

No shadows on diffuse surfaces漫反射表面没有阴影

Weaker under point lights, and shadows can be mistaken for geometry (right). A shadow cache after stage 1, as in GS-IR, is a possible fix.点光源下效果较差，阴影还可能被误当成几何（右图）。可行的改进是像 GS-IR 那样在阶段一之后缓存阴影。

03

Ambiguity remains歧义仍然存在

Light, material and geometry trade off; the material can still be far off in some cases.光照、材质和几何之间可以互相抵消；某些情况下材质仍会偏离很多。

04

Not interactive还不能实时

05

Diffuse scenes: comparable, not best漫反射场景：相当，但不是最好

Take-away要点

RISE-SDF reconstructs glossy objects into geometry, material and lighting that can be relit end to end, with no Blender export and no per-light training.RISE-SDF 把高光物体重建成可端到端重打光的几何、材质和光照，不需要导出到 Blender，也不需要为每种光照单独训练。

1

Share what the two branches have in common. Blend weight ↔ metallic and diffuse colour ↔ albedo come from the same MLPs, which stabilizes material and geometry.让两个分支共享共同的东西。混合权重 ↔ 金属度、漫反射颜色 ↔ 反照率由同一组 MLP 输出，材质和几何都因此更稳定。

2

Split-sum everywhere, including one secondary bounce for relighting: noise-free and faster than Monte Carlo.处处用 split-sum，重打光时的一次二次反弹也是：没有噪声，比 Monte Carlo 更快。

3

Measure material, not just pictures. A glossy dataset with ground-truth albedo, roughness and relighting.评测材质，而不只是评测图像。一个带反照率、粗糙度和重打光真值的高光数据集。

Open problems named in the paper论文中提到的开放问题

  • Shadows on diffuse surfaces (a shadow cache after stage 1)漫反射表面的阴影（在阶段一之后缓存阴影）
  • Near-field light from the surroundings of real captures真实拍摄中周围环境带来的近场光照
  • The photographer's reflection on the object拍摄者自己在物体上的反射
  • Anisotropic and transparent materials各向异性和透明材质
